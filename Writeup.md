##### **High-Energy-Payload-Launcher**



This is a Floating Arm Trebuchet I tried to implement after learning that it is one of the most mechanically efficient designs. It channels the vertical drop of the counterweight fully into the motion of it's arm. 



The stress simulation was conducted with 100kg load and 1000kg counterweights. It passed with Minimum Safety Factor of 2.844 well above the 2.00 baseline. The lowest score of 2.844 occurred at the shaft of the counterweights. 



Bending occurred primarily along the shaft of the counterweight and the primary arm. In fact, the first version failed as the shaft was too thin. Most contact pressure occurred due to the glide wheels. It was also confirmed that the 4 pillars and diagonal braces successfully act as a rigid load-bearing frame with high reaction forces at the base.
